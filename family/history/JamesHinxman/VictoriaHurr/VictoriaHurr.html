Victoria Ann Hurr (nee James)

Born 11th December 1976 in Warrington hospital, Cheshire.

Early years in Lymm, Cheshire, before moving in 1990 to Bramber, West Sussex.

Obtained a BSc in molecular biology from UMIST (before it merged with the University of Manchester) and then a PhD from the John Innes Centre in Norwich. Then became a post-doc at the University of Florida.

  • Married Brandon Hurr in Florida on 13th January 2007 and bought a house in Gainsville.
  • After Bandon completed his PhD he got a job with Syngenta in the UK. They bought a house in Binfield, near Bracknell.
  • Austin was born in May 2012.
  • Syngenta then moved them to northern California in 2014 and they bought a house in Davis.
  • Juniper was born in September 2015.
  • Brandon decided to take a job with the research part of a 'vertical farm' in New Jersey in 2022. They rented a house in Summit and then bought a house in Berkley Heights.
  • Towards the end of 2024, Brandon's company went out of business, so he accepted a job in Canada, on th Niagara Peninsular. However, after six month he decided to accept a job with another vertical farm in Fayetteville, Arkansas. So they bought a house in Fayetteville.
  • At the start of August 2026 that company went out of business! So, at the time of writing, Brandon is looking for another job.
